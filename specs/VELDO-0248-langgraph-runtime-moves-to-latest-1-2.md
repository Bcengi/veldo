## Intent

Owner question, Telegram 39083 (2026-11-04): "Is 1.2 latest?". The lead's answer, 39084: Veldo is locked
to langgraph 1.2.12 (lock and records read 2026-09-23) and the latest is 1.2.19 (PyPI, 2026-10-30). The
move is deliberate: regenerate the lock and its records, run the gate, land. It comes before the workflow
tickets VELDO-0241 to VELDO-0247 are built, so they are built and judged on the runtime Veldo will ship.

## Out of scope

Any change to the adapter, the workflow runner, the activation checks or the approved license set; a
LangGraph 1.3 or 2.x move; the workflow tickets VELDO-0241 to VELDO-0247; removing the 1.2.12 runtime
directory from an account.

## What the reviewer judges

- Normal use: the lock, records and closure are regenerated from the registry, not edited by hand, and
  reproduce from the cached registry reads in proof/VELDO-0248; activation accepts the new runtime and every
  graph and workflow suite passes with no edit.
- Threat model: a wheel that differs from the registry or its RECORD; a new or relicensed package entering
  without the owner's approval; a records file left bound to the old lock.
- Out of review scope (filed, not blocking): behaviour changes inside LangGraph that no existing suite
  exercises.
