## Finding 1 (medium): the event feed and the workers read show a universal-scope colleague the ids of the owner's conversation and of its turn's run

AC1: "Any other caller, a member of the same domain among them, ... sees no conversation."

`.veldo/control_api_models_scope.py:39` (engine copy the same): `conversation_of` recognises a `dispatch_active`
index only while its `dispatch_id` is a string. When a turn's run ends, control_dispatch releases the index with
`dispatch_id=None` (control_dispatch.py:563), so the released index is no longer a conversation record, `reason`
falls through to `if self.universal: return None`, and a universal-scope reader is served it. Its id is
`dispatch-active:["<domain>",null,"<owner's conversation id>","turn"]`. In the same journal entries the turn's
`subscription_reservation` records (ids naming the conversation as unit and project, and the turn's dispatch id)
and the turn's `intake_source` are not treated as conversation records at all
(`.veldo/control_api_authority.py:529` filters per change, and `reason` passes those kinds to a universal reader).

Reproduced: a temporary print in suite 133 after `theirs_events` (restored with git checkout) listed in the
colleague's feed (project_owner, universal scope) `dispatch-active:[...,null,"c-7ffb...","turn"]`,
`reservation:worker:[...,"dispatch/c-7ffb.../b3d8..."]`, `reservation:policy:[...,"unit","c-7ffb..."]`,
`reservation:invocation:[...]` and one `intake_source:...`, where `c-7ffb...` is the owner's conversation and
`dispatch/c-7ffb.../b3d8...` its turn's run. Only ids and kinds are on the feed: no message, reply or record line
leaked (the owner's words were not in the colleague's feed). The released index is also served with its data on
the `workers` read model by the same `reason` path (read from the code, not run). Suite 133's row AC1/events
(scripts/suites/133_veldo_0178_conversation_api.py:624) checks only the conversation, turn and dispatch entity ids,
so it stays green.

A fix round must: decide every record of a conversation turn's run by the conversation's owner, at least the
released `dispatch_active` index (its id or `unit`/`station` name the conversation even after release), the turn's
reservations and the turn's intake source; and make AC1/events assert that the colleague's feed carries no entity
id naming the owner's conversation or the turn's dispatch.
