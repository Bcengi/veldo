## Design review against the owner's bar (a first-class control center)

Judged from all 108 screenshots in `screens/`. The base is clean: one accent, a consistent lamp
language, honest named refusals, a real phone layout with a bottom bar, and dark mode that mostly
works. But as built it is a careful admin page. Nothing on it tells the owner, at a glance, what the
factory is doing and what needs him.

Hierarchy
- There is no overview. The landing screen is a flat runs list. A control center opens on the
  factory's state: running, blocked, failed and waiting-on-you counts, the hosts, and the oldest
  pending decision. Make `/` that overview, with the runs list and the inbox as its two main panels.
- The run screen's header (`runs-*`, `run-*`) is the unit id plus a raw dispatch hash. Show the run's
  state, elapsed time, engine and model, host, and the station as its headline. Move the dispatch id
  to a copyable detail.
- The decisions inbox gives every row the same amber lamp and the same due line, so nothing
  separates urgent from routine. Rank rows by deadline and risk, show "due in 3 d", and colour the
  lamp by urgency.
- On desktop the detail pane is an empty dashed box (`decisions-inbox-1440-light`). Open the first
  decision automatically and give the list its own scroll, so the page itself does not scroll
  (`decision-open-1280-dark` shows the list scrolled off its header).

Typography
- The condensed display face (Big Shoulders) is used for unit ids and the station labels in the strip,
  where it reads cramped and dated at small sizes (`Ready Claimed Dispatch`). Keep it for screen titles
  only; use the UI sans, medium weight, for ids and labels, and mono for ids that get copied.
- In the terminal every line repeats an identical `13:37:00` and stacks a grey kind label above the
  content. That costs a third of the vertical space and shows no information. Use a compact gutter
  (seq, relative time +0.4 s, a coloured kind tag inline) and put the content on the same baseline.

Spacing and density
- On phone the terminal viewport is under half the screen (`run-ended-390-dark`): the strip, status
  row, toggles and a full-width search box come first. Collapse the strip to one line, put Follow and
  Raw and search in one compact bar or an overflow sheet, and give the log the rest of the screen.
- The Monaco diff at 390 px wraps each code line over three rows. On phone, use an inline (unified)
  diff with no wrapping and horizontal scroll inside the diff only.
- Desktop pages use about 40 percent of the width (You, the error states and sign-in at 1280 and
  1440). Use the space, or centre a deliberate measure.

States
- The run error state (`run-error-390-light`) still shows Follow (on), Raw and Search over a refusal.
  Hide every record control when nothing is shown. "Try again" on `unauthorized:out_of_scope` cannot
  succeed; offer it only for retryable refusals (unavailable, network).
- After `stale_presentation` (`decision-refused-390-light`), Send answer stays live and the refusal
  fills a third of the phone screen inside the pinned bar. Replace the action with "Load the current
  version", keep the refusal compact, and clear the choice.
- On phone the open decision (`decision-open-360-light`) pins Send answer over the first choice, and
  none of the choices is visible on arrival. Put the choices above the fold, collapse the Authority
  text, and pin the bar only once a choice is picked.
- The Authority text shows internal identifiers to the owner (`admission_authority, ...`,
  `assignment:e2e-repository:de3`). Show a plain line and put the raw text behind "details".
- Loading skeletons are four grey bars for every screen. Shape them like the content (rows with a lamp
  and a strip; terminal lines with a gutter).
- In dark mode, Send answer is a pale mint with dark text that reads as disabled, and the selected
  inbox row is a muddy olive. Rework the dark accent and the selected-row tokens.
- The strip says "Completed" while its last station is labelled "Done", and the runs list says
  "Ended". Use one word.

Shell
- The rail shows Runs, Decisions and You, plus a theme toggle that the You screen duplicates. Put the
  factory name and host health in the rail. Drop the duplicate toggle. Show the decisions count as a
  real badge, which on phone is currently a bare dot.
- The passkey screen is the strongest page. Keep it, and size the fingerprint comparison for reading
  aloud against `veldo factory passkey`.

Proof hygiene
- The screenshots were taken after other tests ran, so the inbox fills with harness leftovers
  (`Decide dx261114.`, `Revised: admit dl38221 ...`) and the decisions count changes from shot to shot
  (11, 12, 13). Take the screenshots from a fresh harness with curated data, so the proof shows the
  product and not the test residue.
