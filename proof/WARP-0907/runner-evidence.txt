=====================================================================
.warp/supervisor.py imports no subprocess at module top (only `os`, `sys`, `datetime`).
The ONLY subprocess use is lazy, inside RealSystemctl.run, and it only ever runs
`systemctl --user <args>` - never a session, never any other program.
No Popen / os.fork / os.exec* / os.system / os.spawn* anywhere in the module.

Off by default and opt-in:
  - importing supervisor.py and NOT calling install() creates no timer and no service unit.
  - install/status/uninstall are reached ONLY via the explicit `warp supervisor` subcommand.
  - `warp supervisor` with no action and `warp supervisor install` with no schedule both
    fail loud (nonzero), touching no systemd.
