## VELDO-0145 inbox.ts change (OPEN.md item B)

- Defect real: confirmed directly. `control_api_models.SCAN.scan_text('team-amend-f2256b243f6d78a8a93b02af-v2')`
  reports a 38 character high-entropy token, and `redact` masks a `request_id` carrying it. VELDO-0162 builds
  that alias in control_team_routes.py:80. With `request_id` masked the old join in `pendingDecisions` could
  not bind the receipt, so a team request from another member was not answerable in the decisions screen,
  which AC2 requires.
- Fix correct: the fallback applies only when the API's `redacted` list names that receipt's field, and then
  binds by the receipt's own entity id, which control_channel_presentation.py:218-220 and :591 fix as
  `presentation:<channel>:<request>:<version>:<digest>`, rebuilt from the assignment id, request version and
  brief digest. It cannot bind another request's receipt; if the parts do not match it binds nothing (safe).
  Unredacted receipts take the unchanged path, and the decisions browser file stays green.
- Footprint: NOT NEEDED as an addition. engine/ui/src/features/decisions/inbox.ts already lies under the
  spec's declared `engine/ui/**`, so no footprint addition needs confirming. The change itself is needed and
  is in scope for AC2.
