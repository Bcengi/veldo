## Findings

1. BLOCKER - AC2 not met. scripts/fixtures/lifecycle_census.py:491
   unconditionally marks every receipt INCOMPLETE. OPEN.md lists four items as
   REMAINING:
   - exhaustive family/protocol/operation probes;
   - io_uring effect decoding;
   - compat/socketcall;
   - implicit addresses, races and exact socket lifetimes.

   These are all core AC2 Completeness requirements, not scope drift. The
   OPEN items are correctly judged REMAINING and cannot be waived by this
   review.

2. BLOCKER - AC4 consumer propagation not met, and the spec has a defect.
   AC4 requires driving the stand-down through VELDO-0113, 0114 and 0122. All
   three specs declare depends_on VELDO-0111, so the ticket cannot satisfy AC4
   as written. OPEN.md correctly asks for an owner scope decision. Until the
   owner amends the acceptance text or folds the integration into this ticket,
   AC4 is unmet.

3. MAJOR - census/incomplete-is-not-empty does not test fault handling.
   scripts/suites/113_veldo_0111_census.py:232 checks only
   `status == INCOMPLETE and failures`. Both hold on every run because of the
   unconditional qualification failure at lifecycle_census.py:491.

   Three reviewer mutants each made one injected fault a silent no-op: corrupt
   stop, event loss and ABI mismatch. Every one produced capture_status
   COMPLETE with only the qualification failure, and the row stayed green.

   The fault list at suite:229 is also hard-coded rather than generated from
   FAULTS. AC4 asks for fault cases generated from the collector's states.

   This row would therefore stop protecting anything once the qualification
   gap closes.

4. MINOR - Fail-closed paths are untested or unhandled:
   - The CLONE_UNTRACED escape check (lifecycle_census.py:281-287) has no test.
   - accept/accept4 (nr 288 at :275, 43 absent) has no exit handling. Later
     operations on an accepted socket become "unknown socket" failures rather
     than tracked identities.

   This is conservative, but neither behaviour is documented in OPEN.md.

## What a fix round must change

- Close the AC2 OPEN items, or get an owner-approved, recorded scope change to
  the acceptance text.
- Resolve the AC4 dependency cycle with the owner.
- Strengthen the fault matrix:
  - iterate census.FAULTS;
  - require capture_status INCOMPLETE for every fault;
  - require a failure whose capability/category/state match the fault entry

- Add a mutation that swallows a fault as a retained falsifier.
- Add a CLONE_UNTRACED probe and either handle accept/accept4 or record them
