   - host_inventory() hard-codes `exhaustive=False` (lifecycle_census.py:269).
     socket_destruction and shared_socket_memory are also raised whenever any

   - So no real-host receipt can ever be COMPLETE. Every consumer still stands
     down unconditionally. That is honest, but it is the same outcome as

   - I judge all eight OPEN.md items REMAINING and in scope. Each is a stated

     - io_uring decoding;

     depend on 0111.
   - It proposes an amendment, and no spec was edited.
   - Until the owner approves the amendment or the integrations exist, AC4 is
     unmet.

3. MAJOR, AC4 row does not test fault handling: **fixed.**
   - The suite iterates census.FAULTS (113_veldo_0111_census.py:317-331).
   - For each fault it requires capture_status INCOMPLETE and a failure that

   - For event_loss faults it also requires lost_events > 0.
   - capture_status is independent of qualify(), so the qualification reasons
     cannot mask a swallowed fault.
   - The re-driven corrupt, ABI and loss mutants turn the row red, and so do
     the reviewer's extra drain and denial mutants.

## New findings

5. MINOR: an unbound Unix datagram sender produces a phantom service event,
   at scripts/fixtures/lifecycle_census.py:523-530.
   - Any successful connect, sendto, sendmsg, recv or similar call on a
     type 2/3/10 socket with no recorded address emits
     `implicit_receive_binding` and an implicit_address failure.
   - Reproduced with an AF_UNIX SOCK_DGRAM sender without SO_PASSCRED doing
     sendto to a bound receiver. getsockname() is '' (no binding), yet the
     receipt records a service event and capture INCOMPLETE.
   - This fails closed, but the event itself is false. Any ordinary syslog-style
     client would also turn a capture INCOMPLETE.
   - The census/unsupported-address-and-compat row only tests the SO_PASSCRED
     case, which really does autobind.

6. NOTE: FAULTS is a hand-curated table (lifecycle_census.py:46-54), not
   derived from the collector's state machine.
   - It currently covers every state: preflight, arming, collecting and
     draining.
   - No check enforces that coverage, so a future state could be added without
     a fault.

## What a fix round must change

  universe and amended lifetime and race obligations, or implementations for
  the eight named limitations.
