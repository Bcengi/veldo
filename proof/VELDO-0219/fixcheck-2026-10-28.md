## Can the batch measure AC4 as written in OPEN.md?

The contract itself is measurable:
- The scheduler receipt carries every node's start, end, verdict, CPU seconds and anonymous peak,
  plus the critical path, ledger events, capacities and peak grants.
- The mutation receipt carries admissions, peak workers, mean busy cores and per-worker deadlines.
- Both qualification checkers bind the capture to the commit, the forced-fresh run, the host and
  the lock.

  natural way is to apply the registered mutant to a disposable authority copy.

  possible until that is fixed.
